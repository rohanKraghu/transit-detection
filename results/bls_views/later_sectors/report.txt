TOI hosts folded at the pipeline's own BLS ephemeris
========================================================================

Trained on 1402 TOI hosts of sectors 1-13,27-102 (811 planet hosts; 138 taken from a later sector than their first).
Scored on 746 TOI hosts of sectors 14-26 (376 CP/KP hosts, 370 FP/FA-only hosts), no star shared with training.
Hosts without data near their BLS transit, left out: 0 in training, 0 scored.
Centroid test at the BLS ephemeris placed the dip for 85% of training hosts and 80% of scored ones.
Each model here is the mean of 5 boosting fits (seeds 0 to 4).
