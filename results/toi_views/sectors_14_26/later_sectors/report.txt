TOI hosts as folded views, with depth and the centroid test
========================================================================

Trained on 1330 labelled TOIs on 1271 stars of sectors 14-102 (762 planet TOIs; 148 stars taken from a later sector than their first).
Scored on 824 TOI hosts of sectors 1-13 (450 CP/KP hosts, 374 FP/FA-only hosts; 914 TOIs folded), no star shared with training.
Centroid test at the catalogue ephemeris placed the dip for 77% of training TOIs and 83% of scored ones.
Each view model is the mean of 5 boosting fits (seeds 0 to 4); a star scores as its highest TOI.
