TOI hosts as folded views, with depth and the centroid test
========================================================================

Trained on 904 labelled TOIs on 835 stars of sectors 1-13 (517 planet TOIs; 12 stars taken from a later sector than their first).
Scored on 723 TOI hosts of sectors 14-26 (366 CP/KP hosts, 357 FP/FA-only hosts; 780 TOIs folded), no star shared with training.
Centroid test at the catalogue ephemeris placed the dip for 84% of training TOIs and 76% of scored ones.
Each view model is the mean of 5 boosting fits (seeds 0 to 4); a star scores as its highest TOI.
